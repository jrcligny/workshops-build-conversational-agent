
# Definition of invoice statuses
Version 2.0 — audience: accountants — Maison Claire

## Codes and labels
- pending: invoice received, pending approval.
- approved: the approval was submitted after confirmation.
- paid: the invoice has already been paid.

## Matching
Before approving, the accountant matches the invoice, the order and the goods receipt. Common sense says the "paid" status code forbids a new approval, but the system does not prevent a duplicate from being submitted if the user confirms it: the case of a duplicate invoice that has already been paid must be detected by the control designed for that purpose.

## Audience
This sheet is meant for accountants. It is not intended for customer service representatives.
