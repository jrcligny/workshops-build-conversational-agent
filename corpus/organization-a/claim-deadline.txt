
# Processing time of a claim
Version 1.3 — audience: customer service representatives — Maison Claire

## Standard processing time
A simple claim (incomplete delivery, quantity error, damaged parcel) is processed within ten working days from the day it is opened.

## Processing time of a claim for a delay
When there is also a delivery delay, the processing time remains ten working days, but an interim acknowledgement is sent to the customer within forty-eight hours.

## What this sheet does not say
This sheet describes what should happen. The actual status of an order or of a claim is read in the management application, not in this documentation.
