
# Procedure — Incomplete deliveries
Version 2.1 — audience: customer service representatives — Maison Claire

## Purpose
This procedure describes what a customer service representative must do when a delivery arrives incomplete, that is, when some of the lines of an order were not received.

## Findings
The customer service representative notes the order number, the list of missing lines and the date of the partial delivery. They promise no credit note before checking the actual status of the order in the application.

## Claim deadline
The claim for an incomplete delivery must be opened within five working days after the recorded date of the partial delivery. After that deadline, the file is referred for an exceptional review.

## Next steps
1. Open a claim linked to the order.
2. Tell the customer the processing time given in the sheet "Processing time of a claim".
3. Do not change the status of the order: that update belongs to the logistics team.

## Agreed lead time exceeded
When the partial delivery also exceeds the agreed lead time (late delivery), the customer service representative also reports the delay in the claim, without taking over the logistics follow-up.
