
# Approval workflow of a purchase request
Version 3.0 — audience: buyers — Maison Claire

## Preparation
The buyer prepares a draft purchase request: subject, supplier, estimated amount. The draft has no effect until it is submitted.

## Approval
- Up to 1,000 euros: the buyer submits the request, which is assigned to the agent if the agent prepared the draft, on behalf of the buyer.
- Above 1,000 euros: a second review by a purchasing manager is required after submission.

## Prohibition
A customer service representative or a user without write permission neither prepares nor submits a purchase request.
